# L3b: panels (F8, F12 for three files, F14 to F17)

**Goal:** Settings, the Chronicon and the phone page read and behave better: a two-click reset, Settings grouped by
task, keyboard and ARIA on the parchment panels, a clear empty state, themed chart colours, a phone empty state.

## Items

1. **F8** `settings.js`: *Reset to defaults* arms on the first click (label from `prefs.resetArmed`) and resets on a
   second one within 4 s, like *Regenerate token*. Test: one click resets nothing; two reset; a second click after the
   window only re-arms. The existing reset tests click twice (same assertions).
2. **F14** `index.html`: each Settings category regrouped into fieldsets by task (the look of the hall, light, view and
   size, clicks; petitions, quiet hours, vigil, stuck sessions, token alert, day report; hall plan, rooms; window,
   performance, folders, updates). "Theme" becomes **World** and "Style" **Look** (`prefs.world`, `prefs.look`; the
   `name="world"` and `name="theme"` controls and their meaning stay). Selects stop truncating at 800 px
   (`panels.css`). Test: every setting key (from `settings`, `MONITOR`, and the other defaults) has exactly one control.
3. **F15** `panel.js` and `index.html`: `aria-modal="true"` on `#chron` and `#prefs`; Tab and Shift+Tab cycle inside an
   open panel; Esc closes and returns focus to the opener. `tablist()` in `panel.js`: Left/Right (and Up/Down), Home/End
   move between tabs, roving `tabindex`. Used by the Chronicon day tabs (move focus, Enter selects) and the Settings
   categories (the move selects). The categories become a `role="tablist"` inside `nav.cats`; *Reset* stays outside it.
   Tests: `panel.test.mjs` (new), tab-list arrow tests in `chronicon.test.mjs` and `settings.test.mjs`.
4. **F16** `chronicon.js`, `panels.css`: an empty day shows `log.empty` in a styled empty state; the event icons and the
   new/cache bars take their colours from `--chart-*` (icon letters become classes, no hex in the markup); the cache
   part carries a stripe pattern and a lighter tone, so it no longer relies on red against green. Test: empty day text;
   no hex in an icon; two themes give different `--chart-*` and the CSS maps every icon letter to a variable.
5. **F17** `mobile.js`, `mobile.html`, `mobile.css`: `mobile.empty` when no petition waits; a risky petition's row gets
   the `risky` class (red, like the PC's label); targets at least 44 px. The phone never offers Approve on a risky
   petition (`petition.js` `allowed`, Deny only), so the class goes on the row, not on an Approve that is not there.
6. **F12** `settings.js`, `chronicon.js`, `mobile.js` use `dom.js` `el` and `format.js` `hhmm`, `ago`, `statusOf`; their
   own copies go.
7. **Text keys** `theme.js` `TEXT`: `prefs.resetArmed`, `prefs.world`, `prefs.look` (replacing `prefs.theme`),
   `log.empty`, `mobile.empty`, `petition.armed` (for L3a: the armed Approve). `docs/theme-packs.md` lists them.
   `LOCKED` unchanged.
