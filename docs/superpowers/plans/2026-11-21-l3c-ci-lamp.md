# L3c I6 lamp: plan

Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-21-ci-verdict-design.md` section 5 (the lamp, one per folder); joint spec section 4 L3c.
Backend (merged in W2): the `ci-lamps` event and the `ci_lamps` command, `[{ dept, cwd, state, branch, sha7, failed }]`,
`state` one of `running`, `pass`, `fail`, `timeout`, `error`, `none`. An empty list when the feature is off.

## Items

1. **Art family `CILAMP`.** Frames `CILAMP.off`, `.amber`, `.amber_dim`, `.green`, `.red`, 10 x 20 art px (5 x 10 logical),
   a signal post: base, pole, housing with one round lens. Base art only (every world uses it until it redraws it; no
   `theme.json` change). Generator `tools/cilamp_art.mjs` + `tools/cilamp_art/{kit,tier2}.mjs` (the lift / honours
   pattern), so the art gate reruns it. Registered in `PROP_SHEETS` (`ui/sprites.js`), sizes in `ui/sprites.test.mjs`,
   listed last in `tools/sprite_catalog.mjs`. Its 39° form: a flat billboard standing upright on its projected foot
   (like the skull and the flames), so no faces entry.
2. **`ui/cilamp.js`, pure state.** `listen('ci-lamps')` plus one `invoke('ci_lamps')` at start; `lamps()` (the list),
   `lampOf(dept)`, `frameOf(state, t)` (running: amber / amber_dim twice a second-pair, one blink a second; pass green;
   fail red; else off). Test `ui/cilamp.test.mjs`: states to frames, the blink with a fake time, one entry per
   department, two departments in one room give two lamps, an empty list draws nothing.
3. **Placement `lampSpots(blocks, H)` in `ui/layout.js`**: one spot (foot centre, logical px) per department block, by
   the plan in force: open hall, rooms plan, west wing rooms and the break-out room on the block's top-left, in the 8 px
   band over its desks (behind them, clear of desks, consoles and the lanes); strip and column on the plaque row.
   Tests in `ui/layout.test.mjs`.
4. **Drawing.** `drawScene` (flat, also the strip and the column, which draw through it) and `drawScene39` push one
   y-sorted item per lamp that has an entry in the list; the contact shadow like other props. Nothing when empty.
5. **Catalog and galleries.** Regenerate `docs/sprites/` (default and every theme), `node tools/art_gate.mjs` clean.
