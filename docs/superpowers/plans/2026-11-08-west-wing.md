# West wing (O10c) — design and vertical-slice plan

Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-08-custom-rooms-design.md`, section O10c (binding; O10b superseded).

## Design

The custom rooms leave the scriptorium. While at least one rule exists, a **west wing** stands west of the
scriptorium: a column of real rooms, the mirror of the Refectorium/Sanctum column, stacked in rule order.

- **Geometry.** `hallOf(bays, size, wing)` builds the hall as today for the window and, with a wing, shifts every x
  of it east by `wx` (the wing and the scriptorium's new west wall). The scene is `wx` wider; nothing else of the
  scriptorium changes. `hall.base` is the unshifted hall: the flat background draws it under `translate(wx, 0)`, the
  39° plan shifts its surfaces by `2 wx`, `propsOf` / `staticLights` shift theirs. So a hall without a wing is the
  same object as before (the flathall / isohall hashes hold), and no prop literal moves.
- **Rooms.** `wing` is the layout's room list `[{ id, color, empty, rows }]` (`layoutDepartments` fills it);
  `hallOf` turns it into `hall.rooms`, each in the zone shape the drawing code already reads
  (`{ kind: 'room', id, color, empty, x, y, w, h }`) plus its wall band, lanes, corridor and door.
- **Layout.** `zoneSequence` is unchanged (the strip still uses its placeholders). `layoutDepartments` sends every
  room entry (members and an empty room's placeholder) into its wing room and packs the rest in the scriptorium as
  before; `zonesOf` keeps only the break-out room.
- **Routes.** `roomOf` names a wing room `w:<id>`. Inside a room the same lane/corridor walk runs on the room's own
  lanes and corridor; a room is left and entered only through its door, whose hall side stands on a scriptorium lane.
- **Drawing.** Flat: the wing's walls and floors in `drawStatic`, its doors in `drawDoors` (the refectorium's door
  frames, animated by `doorFrame`), the dyed `ENCLAVE_FLOOR` per room by `zoneFloor`. 39°: the wing's floor, north
  wall and west wall, the scriptorium's west wall a cutaway slab with a gap and jambs per door, a slab per room
  divider.
- **Plaques.** One department: its own `b:` plaque on the room's top wall, in the rule colour. Several: the room's
  `r:` plaque on the top wall, department plaques inside. Empty: the rule's name, dimmed.

## Rulings

Ruling: wing width — the wing is 138 px (the east column's `RIGHT_W`) plus the scriptorium's 8 px west wall in
`wall dark` (the east wall's art and thickness): `wx = 146`, the scriptorium's west edge.
Ruling: hall width — `hall.w` = the window's scene width + `wx`; the scriptorium keeps its own width rules
(`MAX_W`, the Scale setting) and the view pans to the wing as it pans to bays. `viewport.js` is unchanged.
Ruling: how hallOf/w accounts for the wing — `hallOf(bays, size, wing)` = `hallOf(bays, size)` with every x field
(+ `x0, x1, sw, rx, ox, dx, corridorX, entry, doorOut/In, refOut/In, recaff, refectory, cogSpots, queue`) moved by
`wx`; `hall.wx` (0 without a wing), `hall.base` (the unshifted hall), `hall.rooms`; every cache keyed on the
hall's size also keys on the wing (`hall.key`).
Ruling: the wing flag — explicit: `planLayout` gets `rooms` (`rooms.list` in the hall views, `[]` in the column);
the wing exists while `rooms.length > 0`, never inferred from the sequence.
Ruling: room height per desk rows — the wing uses the scriptorium's slot-row grid (rows at `y0 + 64 k`), 2 desks a
row (4 lecterns compact) west of the room's corridor at x 128; a room holds as many rows as its departments need, at
least one. The first room's desks start on row 0 under the back wall; each further room starts on the next grid row
(no row lost, review F4) under a 10 px `wall dark` divider (the refectorium/sanctum's) packed into the floor above its
first desk row: from 2 px under the lane above to the desks' top; its rugs start at the desks' top. Below the last
room: `wall dark` then plated `wall` down to the hall's bottom. Probe at the minimum hall (level 0 full desks, 1
compact, 1 + b bays): two rules with one one-desk department each, or both empty, level 2 before, 0 after; three
rules, level 4 before, 2 after.
Ruling (2026-11-09, spec O10c.9, supersedes the heights above and the door places below): full height — the rooms
share the wing's full height on the slot-row grid. Each room gets its desk rows (`wing[].rows`) plus
`floor(spare / n)` of the hall's spare rows; the last room takes `spare % n` more and runs down to `hall.h`. The
result is `hall.rooms[].rows`. `hallOf` alone does this split: `layoutDepartments` packs each room's blocks from its
top and places them at the `k` that `hallOf` gives. Nothing is drawn below the last room (flat `drawWing`; 39° no
`wing below` slab).
Ruling (2026-11-09): door centred — a room's door is on its lane (`R.lanes`, every one a scriptorium lane) whose
door centre (`lane - 8`) is nearest the room's vertical middle. On a tie, the lower lane. The routing is unchanged.
Ruling (2026-11-09, coordinator): the break-out room is packed as at b8927f1 (below the lane under the last room's
first desk row, desk rows alone). Each door is the lane nearest the room's middle outside the break-out fence
(`wing[].door`, chosen by `layoutDepartments`, taken by `hallOf`). If the even share would leave a room with no
such lane, the share is capped (`wing[].share`) so that every room starts above the fence.
Ruling (2026-11-09): a scribe in a room that loses rows under it moves to that room's nearest lane. A scribe in the
west wall moves to its door's new hall side (`Cast.resized`).
Ruling: capacity — the wing's rows count against the hall's rows like any block: past them a
room's departments overflow, so the hall goes compact, then grows bays, as today. A room absent from the sequence
(its placeholder dropped past `MAX_BAYS`) is not drawn: an empty room never pushes a live department into overflow.
Ruling: door position — one door per room in the scriptorium's west wall (x `wx - 8 .. wx`), on the scriptorium lane
just above the first room's desks (`y0`), just under every other room's first desk row (`y0 + 60 + 64 k`), 20 px tall,
ending 2 px under its lane (`y - 18 .. y + 2`, inside the room's band). Its frames are each world's existing `door refectory` frames,
unflipped (no new art), opening while anyone is within 12 px (`near`, `doorFrame` with key `w:<id>`).
Ruling: routing — the door's hall side `(wx + 1, y)` and room side `(128, y)` (the room's corridor) take only their
own lane (as the corridor does); a walk inside a room is the hall's lane/corridor walk on the room's lanes. A point
in the west wall (`wx - 8 .. wx + 1`, a scribe crossing a door when its route is recomputed) keeps to its door's
lane, out through its hall side or in through its room side (review F1).
Ruling: the wing's cache key holds geometry only (id, rows); a room's colour and emptiness come from the layout's
blocks (`wingZones`), so filling, emptying or recolouring a room rebuilds no background and re-routes nobody (F2).
Ruling: 39° ambient occlusion — the hall's own bands moved east with it (none across the wing), plus the wing's back
wall, each divider and both faces of the scriptorium's west wall (F7).
Ruling: the wing coming or going keeps the view on the same floor point of the scriptorium (`app.js` re-fits on it,
F9); checked on screen.
Ruling: 39° projection — unchanged; the world box widens by the wing (`U = 2 w`). The base plan's west wall moves to
the wing's west edge; the scriptorium's west wall is a cutaway slab broken at each door with a jamb post either side
(as the east wall); each divider a slab; below the last room a slab
(as the bays' east of the sanctum). No door leaves (the hall's own 39° doorways have none).
Ruling: props — the wing has none of its own (desks, consoles, rugs only); no prop of the scriptorium moves relative
to it.
Ruling: plaques — a one-department room's plaque is that department's `b:` plaque (click opens its folder, branch
shown) on the room's top wall in the rule colour; `r:<id>` for several departments and for an empty room (dim).
Ruling: resize — when `wx` changes (first rule added, last removed), every actor in the scriptorium and the east
column steps by the change (`Cast.resized`, also triggered by a `wx` change).
Ruling: empty rooms and capacity — an empty room that does not fit counts one toward the overflow, so the hall goes
compact and grows bays to keep its place, even with few departments (the spec's accepted cost); past `MAX_BAYS` its
placeholder is dropped and the overflow counts live sessions only.
Ruling: the break-out room starts below every wing door's lane (`layoutDepartments`), so a walk from a door along
its lane never crosses the break-out fence. (Superseded 2026-11-09: no door lane inside its fence, see above.)
Ruling: a room changing height moves whoever stands in the rooms below it by the rows their room moved
(`SLOT_H * Δk`, `Cast.resized`, triggered by a change of `hall.rooms`); whoever stood in a deleted room, or in the
wing that went, stands at that room's door, hall side (F3). A hall without a wing shares one frozen empty `rooms`
list, so it never looks re-roomed.
Ruling: empty wing rooms keep their placeholders (`zoneSequence`), which hold their place (F5; spec O10c.7 updated).

Open (not a ruling): spec O10c.7 keeps the enclave fence frames generated only if the strip still draws them. The
strip draws `ENCLAVE_FLOOR` and `ENCLAVE_GATE_FRONT` / `_GATE_FRONT_OPEN`; nothing draws `ENCLAVE_RAIL`, `_SIDE`,
`_POST`, `_GATE`, `_GATE_OPEN` any more (the hall has no room zone; the 39° fence's room branch is dead code).
Removing them touches `ui/art/**/enclave.*`, `tools/enclave_art*`, `tools/sprite_catalog.mjs` and `art.test.mjs`'s
enclave checks, outside this change's write set: the parent decides (authorise the art and generator change, or keep).

## Vertical slices (each green before the next)

1. Shifted hall: `hallOf(..., wing)`, `hall.base`, cache keys, `propsOf` / `staticLights` shifted, `Cast.resized`.
   Test: every x of a winged hall is the base's + `wx`, every y the same (mutate one field: red).
2. Wing layout and routes: `layoutDepartments` packs rooms in the wing, `hall.rooms`, `roomOf` `w:<id>`, `route`
   through doors. Tests: rule order whatever the arrival order; an empty room keeps its place; a live department
   never pushed into overflow by an empty room; walkers enter and leave rooms only through their doors; walls never
   cover a desk, seat, queue slot or prop; break-out room unchanged.
3. Flat drawing: walls, doors, floors, plaques. Tests: a door open while someone is on its frame; overlay plaques.
   Screenshots, then flathall hashes for the winged hall recorded.
4. 39°: `roomPlan`, `scene39` floors. Tests: isohall surfaces; screenshot; isohall hashes recorded.
5. Docs (README, DEVELOPMENT), sprite catalogue / galleries if any frame's use changed, art gate.
